Privacy

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no accounts. The pages load fonts and images only from this site. Your host may keep ordinary server logs (IP address, time, page) for security and capacity.

The app

Nekoizer processes your microphone on your PC and does not upload audio or send usage data. It connects to the internet only to download its voice models (from Hugging Face), to fetch a link you import, to open voice-models.com when you press that button, and to fetch the installer parts when you install. Reference clips and RVC voices you add stay on your PC under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Nekoizer.

Third parties

Donations go through Ko-fi, which has its own privacy policy. Downloads of models come from Hugging Face, installer parts from GitHub, both under their own policies.
