What I make

Nekoizer

Windows app · available

Real-time voice changer. Load a clip or an RVC voice and talk as someone else in Discord, games and calls.

Fretwork

VST3 plugin · built, not released

A physically modelled guitar instrument for your DAW: strings, bodies, pickups and articulations.

Namrig

VST3 plugin · early development

An amp and cabinet rig effect with a tone stack and tube stage. Neural amp models are not wired in yet.

attmusic

Desktop app · built, not released

A music player with a radiooooo-style discovery view.
