Namrig VST3 plugin · early development

An amp and cabinet effect for guitar in your DAW, with the plan of running Neural Amp Modeler files. It is at an early stage, and this page is honest about it.

What works in the code today

  • Tone stack: bass, mid and treble.
  • Gain and mix: input gain, output gain, dry and wet mix, bypass.
  • Cabinet and mic controls: cab mix, mic mix, mic distance and angle.
  • Tube stage controls: tube type (12AX7, 12AT7, 12AU7, 6L6, EL34) and drive.
  • 17 automatable parameters in total, as VST3 and standalone for Windows.

What does not work yet

  • The engine can load a Neural Amp Modeler (.nam) file, but the plugin does not call it yet, so no neural amp model is running.
  • The amp, cab and mic choices are placeholder entries (Model 1 to 4, Cab 1 to 4, Mic 1 to 4).
  • The editor is a plain window of sliders and shows only part of the controls.

Status

The VST3 on my PC is 6.8 MB, built on 6 Nov 2026. There is no public download and no screenshot yet. I would rather show you nothing than a picture of something that does not do what a rig plugin should.
