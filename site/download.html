Download Nekoizer

Download for Windows v1.0.0 · free · small installer

The installer is small. It downloads the app (two parts, about 2.3 GB in total) while it installs, then Nekoizer downloads its voice models (about 1.5 GB) the first time you start it. Plan for about 6 GB of free disk space.

You need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • An NVIDIA graphics card with a current driver (4 GB of video memory works)
  • The free VB-Cable virtual audio cable (vb-audio.com)

About the SmartScreen warning

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway. If you would rather check the file first, upload it to a scanner of your choice. This page will list its SHA-256 when it is published.

Uninstall

Remove Nekoizer in Windows Settings, Apps. Your downloaded models, voices and logs stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Nekoizer. Delete that folder to remove them.
