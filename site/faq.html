Questions

Is Nekoizer really free?

Yes. There is no account, no ad and no paywall. Tips on Ko-fi are optional.

Does my voice leave my PC?

No. Conversion runs on your graphics card. Nekoizer goes online only to download its models, to import a link you paste, and when you open voice-models.com from the button.

Why does Windows warn me?

The installer is not code-signed yet. Code-signing certificates cost money and need identity checks. Choose More info, then Run anyway, if you trust the download.

Can I use any voice?

Technically you can load any clip or RVC model, but you must have the right to use that person's voice. Do not use it to deceive or harass anyone, or to pass yourself off as a real person.

What are RVC voices?

RVC models are small voice files (.pth) trained by the community. Nekoizer loads them in addition to normal reference clips, and keeps both in the same bank.

Are RVC files safe?

They can contain code, so Nekoizer only loads them in a restricted mode and refuses files that do not load that way. Even so, only import models from sources you trust.

Why does the first start take a while?

It downloads about 1.5 GB of voice models. On a slow connection this can take a long time, and it resumes after a break.

It sounds delayed. What can I do?

Close other GPU-heavy apps, lower the quality steps, or use a larger block size. Real-time conversion needs a capable graphics card; RVC voices need more headroom than clip voices.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Nekoizer is Windows only for now.
